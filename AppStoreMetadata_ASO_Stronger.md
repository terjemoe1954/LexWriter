## Stronger App Description

LexWriter helps you create legal-style personal document drafts quickly and clearly on iPhone.

Use ready-made templates for wills, contracts, powers of attorney, rental agreements, purchase agreements, receipts, loan agreements, employment agreements, NDAs, and more. Enter your information, review the result in a clean preview, and save or print the finished draft as a PDF.

LexWriter is built for practical document writing with a calm, structured interface that makes each document type easier to complete. Whether you need a printable agreement, a clear personal record, or a formal draft to review before signing, LexWriter keeps the process simple and organized.

Features:
- Multiple legal-style document templates in one app
- Clear step-by-step editing screens
- Print-ready preview
- Save documents as PDF
- Support for Norwegian, English, and Thai
- Clean light, dark, and system appearance modes

Document types include:
- Will
- Contract
- Power of Attorney
- Rental Agreement
- Cohabitation Agreement
- Debt Instrument
- Purchase Agreement
- Termination of Tenancy
- Receipt
- Loan Agreement
- Employment Agreement
- NDA

LexWriter is intended for personal drafting and document preparation. It does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or law firm services.
