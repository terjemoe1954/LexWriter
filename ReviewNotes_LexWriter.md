## App Description

LexWriter helps users create structured legal-style personal document drafts in a calm and print-friendly workspace.

The app supports Norwegian, English, and Thai, and includes multiple document types such as wills, contracts, powers of attorney, rental agreements, receipts, purchase agreements, loan agreements, and more.

Users can enter their own information, review the result in a clear preview, then print or save the document as PDF.

LexWriter is designed for personal drafting and practical document preparation. The app does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or lawyer-client services.
