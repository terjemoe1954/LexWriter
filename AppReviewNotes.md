# App Review Notes

## Forslag til tekst

LexWriter is a document drafting app for private users. The app helps users prepare structured legal-style document templates, preview them, print them, and export them as PDF files on device.

The app does not provide individualized legal advice, legal representation, or professional review. It provides editable templates and guidance text only.

Core review flow:

1. Open `Settings` to choose language and appearance.
2. Return to the home screen and open any document type.
3. Fill in the required fields.
4. Open preview from the print section.
5. In preview, use `Print` or `Save PDF`.

The app does not require account creation, login, or payment to access its core functionality.

User-entered document data is handled locally in the app workflow. The app currently does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or in-app purchases.

## Norsk intern merknad

Hvis App Review spør om juridisk ansvar eller innhold, svar at appen er et skriveverktøy for standardiserte dokumentutkast og at brukeren selv fyller inn innholdet. Ved komplekse juridiske forhold anbefales profesjonell rådgivning.
