## English

### Subtitle

Legal document templates with PDF

### Promotional Text

Create legal-style document drafts quickly, preview them in a formal layout, and export them to PDF directly from the app.

### Short Description

LexWriter makes it easy to prepare structured legal document drafts with preview, printing, and PDF export.

### Long Description

LexWriter is a practical writing tool for people who want to prepare legal-style document drafts in a fast, clear, and structured way.

Choose a document type, fill in the relevant details, and get a formal layout ready for preview, printing, or PDF export. The app is designed to help users gather the necessary information and structure documents directly on their device.

With LexWriter, you can prepare:

- wills
- contracts
- powers of attorney
- rental agreements
- cohabitation agreements
- promissory notes
- purchase agreements
- tenancy termination notices
- receipts
- loan agreements
- employment agreements
- NDAs

Key features:

- structured legal-style document templates
- preview before printing or signing
- PDF export
- direct printing from the app
- support for Norwegian, English, and Thai
- light mode, dark mode, and system appearance

Important:

LexWriter provides document drafts and standardized templates only. The app does not provide individualized legal advice. For complex matters or important legal decisions, the document should be reviewed by a lawyer or another qualified adviser before use or signing.
